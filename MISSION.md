# Mission

**Money Making Machines** exists to democratize access to automated income.

We believe that financial tools and knowledge should be freely available to
everyone — not locked behind paywalls, proprietary platforms, or exclusive
communities.

Our mission is to build an open, practical encyclopedia of ways to make money
online using automated software, published as **Public Domain (Unlicense)** so
every person on earth can use, copy, modify, and build on it without
restriction.

Every guide we create must:

- **Work manually** — a human can follow each step without any automation
- **Work automatically** — a button runs every step for the user with full
  transparency about what is happening
- **Be understandable** — plain language, no assumed expertise
- **Be honest** — realistic expectations, clear risks, no hype

We are not selling anything. We are not affiliated with any of the platforms or
services we document. We are just building the resource we wish existed.
