AI Data Labeling & RLHF

Get paid to train and evaluate AI models. Companies need humans to rate answers, write examples, and fact-check model output — and in 2026 they pay well for it.

Active Beginner

Overview

The AI boom created huge demand for human feedback. "RLHF" (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) and data labeling tasks ask you to compare two model answers, write ideal responses, fix code, or flag errors. This work is genuinely available today and pays far better than old-style survey/microtask sites.

Realistic earnings: typically $15–$40+ per hour depending on your skills and the project. Specialised expertise (coding, math, law, medicine, a second language) commands the highest rates. This is active work, not passive income — but you choose your own hours.

⚠️ Watch for scams: Legitimate platforms never ask you to pay to start or to "buy equipment". Apply only through official sites and never share banking passwords. Work availability fluctuates, so treat it as flexible income rather than a guaranteed salary.

Step-by-step Guide

1

Pick a reputable platform

These are the most established, currently-paying platforms in 2026:

  • DataAnnotation — dataannotation.tech — writing and reviewing AI training data; pays via PayPal weekly
  • Outlier — outlier.ai — expert RLHF projects (coding, STEM, languages) run by Scale AI
  • Mercor — mercor.com — matches experts to AI training and interview-based gigs
or visit the links above manually
2

Create an account & complete the assessment

Sign up with a real email. Most platforms gate paid work behind a short qualification assessment that mirrors the real tasks — for example, rating two AI responses and explaining which is better, or writing a correct solution to a prompt.

Take the assessment seriously: it determines whether you get access and at what pay tier. Treat it like a job interview.

or register manually
3

List your specialised skills

Pay scales with expertise. In your profile, declare every relevant skill honestly — the higher-paying queues are skill-gated:

  • Programming languages (Python, JavaScript, SQL, Rust…)
  • Math, physics, chemistry, statistics
  • Law, medicine, finance, or other professional fields
  • Fluency in a non-English language
  • Strong writing and editing

A coding or STEM background can move you from the ~$15/hr tier to the $40+/hr tier.

or fill in your profile manually
4

Do the work carefully — quality is tracked

Once approved, projects appear in a dashboard. Typical task types:

  • Preference rating: read two model answers, choose the better one, and justify it
  • Response writing: write the ideal answer to a prompt for the model to learn from
  • Fact-checking & red-teaming: find errors, hallucinations, or unsafe output
  • Code review: verify and fix model-generated code

Platforms continuously score your accuracy. High quality keeps you in the best-paid queues; sloppy work gets you removed. Read each project's instructions in full before starting.

or open your dashboard manually
5

Get paid & scale up

Most platforms pay weekly via PayPal once you pass a small minimum balance. To increase income over time:

  • Sign up to two or three platforms to smooth out dry spells
  • Qualify for more skill areas to unlock higher-pay queues
  • Maintain a high quality score — it directly affects how much work you're offered
