Digital Products & Print-on-Demand

Create something once and sell it forever. Digital downloads and print-on-demand products are fulfilled automatically, so income keeps coming after the upfront work.

Passive Beginner

Overview

With digital products you do the work once and the platform handles delivery and payment forever after. Two complementary models:

  • Digital downloads — templates, e-books, presets, printables, Notion templates, design assets. Near-zero marginal cost; 100% of each sale (minus fees) is profit.
  • Print-on-demand (POD) — t-shirts, mugs, posters, stickers. A partner prints and ships each item only when it sells, so you hold no inventory.

Realistic earnings: highly variable — from a few dollars to a meaningful side income, depending on your designs, niche, and marketing. Most of the work is upfront; the fulfilment is fully automated.

⚠️ Be realistic: Listing a product is automatic, but getting sales takes good designs and some marketing. Avoid uploading copyrighted or trademarked artwork — POD platforms remove infringing listings and can ban your account. Create original work.

Step-by-step Guide

1

Pick a product type & niche

Decide what you'll sell and for whom. A focused niche sells far better than generic products. Examples:

  • Notion / spreadsheet templates for a specific profession
  • Printable planners, wall art, or wedding invitations
  • Lightroom presets or design asset packs
  • POD apparel for a hobby community (e.g. climbers, knitters)

Manual: Browse Etsy and Gumroad for what's already selling in your area of interest to validate demand.

or research manually above
2

Create your product

Make the asset once. Free and low-cost tools cover almost everything:

  • Canva — canva.com — design printables, planners, social templates, and POD artwork
  • Notion / Google Sheets — build template products
  • Your own photos, illustrations, or writing for presets and e-books

Export print-ready files (high-resolution PNG/PDF). For POD, follow your partner's size and DPI guidelines.

or build it manually
3

Choose a selling platform

Match the platform to your product type:

  • Gumroad — gumroad.com — simplest for digital downloads; instant delivery, you own the customer relationship
  • Etsy — etsy.com — huge built-in marketplace traffic for printables and POD
  • Printful — printful.com — POD fulfilment that connects to Etsy/Shopify
  • Printify — printify.com — POD with a wide partner network and often lower base prices
or visit the links above manually
4

List the product & automate fulfilment

Upload your file or connect your POD partner. This is where the automation lives — once listed, delivery is hands-off:

  • Digital: upload the file to Gumroad/Etsy — buyers get it instantly on purchase, automatically
  • POD: connect Printful/Printify to your store; each order is printed and shipped without you touching it

Write a clear title, description, and tags so the marketplace search can find you. Set your price above your fees and base cost.

or create the listing manually
5

Promote & collect payouts

Listings sell better with a little promotion — share on social media, build an email list, or run small ads. Platforms pay out automatically to your bank/PayPal on their schedule. Then scale by adding more products: a catalogue of many small earners compounds over time.
