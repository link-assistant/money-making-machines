Affiliate Content

Build a blog or niche site with AI-assisted content and earn commissions every time a reader buys a product through your links.

Active Intermediate

Overview

Affiliate marketing is one of the most scalable online income models. You create helpful content (blog posts, comparison guides, tutorials) and include affiliate links to products. When a reader clicks and buys, you earn a commission — typically 3–10% for physical goods and 20–50% for digital products.

AI writing tools dramatically reduce content creation time while open-source static site generators (Hugo, Jekyll, Eleventy) give you a free, fast website hosted on GitHub Pages.

⚠️ Disclosure requirement: Most jurisdictions and affiliate programs (including Amazon Associates) require you to clearly disclose that your links are affiliate links. This is both a legal requirement and builds reader trust.

Step-by-step Guide

1

Choose a niche

Pick a topic you know something about that also has products people buy online. The best niches have:

  • Products with decent commissions ($10+ per sale)
  • A clear audience with buying intent
  • Enough sub-topics for 20–50 articles (e.g. "home espresso machines", "budget hiking gear", "Python developer tools")

Use Google Trends or Ahrefs free keyword generator to validate search demand.

or research keywords manually
2

Join affiliate programs

Apply to programs that cover your niche. Start with these:

  • Amazon Associates (affiliate-program.amazon.com) — 3–10%, covers almost every category
  • ShareASale / Impact / CJ Affiliate — networks with thousands of merchants
  • Direct programs — many software companies pay 20–50% recurring commissions (e.g. Semrush, ConvertKit, Webflow)
or sign up manually
3

Set up a free website on GitHub Pages

A static site is free to host and fast to load. Use Hugo (the most popular open-source static site generator): 

# Install Hugo (macOS/Linux via package manager, or download from gohugo.io)
brew install hugo        # macOS with Homebrew
# or: https://gohugo.io/installation/

# Create new site
hugo new site my-affiliate-blog
cd my-affiliate-blog

# Add a theme (e.g. PaperMod)
git init
git submodule add https://github.com/adityatelange/hugo-PaperMod themes/PaperMod
echo 'theme = "PaperMod"' >> config.toml

# Create your first post
hugo new posts/my-first-review.md

# Preview locally
hugo server -D

Push to GitHub and enable Pages in repository Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch (main / docs).

or follow the commands above manually
4

Write SEO-optimised content with AI assistance

AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT) can draft a first pass of an article in minutes. Your job is to add real expertise, personal experience, and up-to-date product data.

Effective article types for affiliate income:

  • "Best [product] for [use case]" — comparison posts
  • "[Product A] vs [Product B]" — head-to-head reviews
  • "How to [task] using [product]" — tutorial posts
  • "[Product] review" — detailed single product reviews

Target keywords with monthly search volume 100–2000 and low difficulty (use Ahrefs free tool).

or write articles manually
5

Insert affiliate links & add disclosure

Replace plain product links with your tracked affiliate links. Add a clear disclosure near the top of every article, for example: 

> **Affiliate disclosure:** Some links on this page are affiliate links.
> If you click through and make a purchase, I may earn a small commission
> at no extra cost to you.

Use a link management plugin or simple markdown anchor tags if using a static site.

or add disclosure text manually
6

Publish, track, & iterate

Add Google Search Console (free) to track which pages get impressions and clicks. After 3 months, identify your top performing pages and write more on those topics.

Typical timeline:

  • Months 1–3: publish 20+ articles, little traffic
  • Months 3–6: Google indexes and starts ranking, first commissions
  • Months 6–12: compounding traffic growth, $100–$500/month target
or track manually
