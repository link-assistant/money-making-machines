Bandwidth Sharing

Earn passive income by sharing the internet bandwidth you're not using. Apps run quietly in the background — no effort after setup.

Passive Beginner

Overview

Bandwidth-sharing apps pay you a small amount per gigabyte of traffic routed through your connection. They are used by companies for web research, price comparison, and ad verification. Typical earnings: $1–$5 per device per month, depending on location and connection speed.

⚠️ Note: Earnings are modest. These apps work best as a set-and-forget background income rather than a primary source. Always read each app's privacy policy before installing.

Step-by-step Guide

1

Check your internet plan

Make sure your internet plan allows bandwidth sharing (most home plans do). Check if you have a data cap — if so, only run the app when you have spare data. Unlimited plans are ideal.

Manual: Log in to your ISP's website or app and review your plan details.

or do it manually above
2

Choose a bandwidth-sharing app

Several reputable apps exist. Running multiple apps multiplies your earnings (they each use separate traffic pools).

  • Honeygain — honeygain.com — most popular, $5 sign-up bonus
  • EarnApp — earnapp.com — by BrightData (trusted company)
  • Peer2Profit — peer2profit.com
  • PacketStream — packetstream.io — $0.10/GB
or visit the links above manually
3

Create accounts & download apps

Sign up with an email address (use a dedicated email if you prefer). Download the desktop or mobile app for each service you chose. Installation is straightforward — next, next, finish.

Docker users: All four services above have Docker images. You can run them all at once using the open-source money4band compose stack. 

# money4band Docker stack (optional, Linux/macOS/Windows with Docker)
git clone https://github.com/NikolaiGulatz/money4band.git
cd money4band
cp .env.example .env
# Edit .env — add your account tokens for each service
docker compose up -d
or follow the instructions above manually
4

Log in to each app & enable auto-start

Open each app, log in with the account you created, and enable "Launch at startup" or "Start on login" in settings. This ensures the app keeps running even after a reboot — which is key for passive income.

or configure manually in each app
5

Monitor earnings & request payout

Check your dashboards weekly. Most services pay out via PayPal, gift cards, or cryptocurrency once you reach a minimum threshold ($20–$50). Set a calendar reminder to request payouts so earnings don't sit idle.

or log in to each app manually
