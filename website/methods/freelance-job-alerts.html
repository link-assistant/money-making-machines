Freelance Job Alerts

Be the first to apply to new freelance jobs by automatically watching Upwork, Freelancer, Guru and PeoplePerHour via RSS feeds. Respond faster = win more contracts.

Active Beginner

Overview

Freelance platforms post new jobs every few minutes. The contractors who respond within the first hour get hired far more often. This guide sets up an RSS-based alert system that notifies you the moment a matching job is posted — without violating any platform Terms of Service (passive monitoring via RSS is always allowed).

Realistic expectations: This won't automate applying or writing proposals (which would risk account bans). It gives you a genuine competitive edge by ensuring you see every relevant job instantly.

⚠️ ToS note: RSS feed reading is always allowed. Automated proposal submission or account creation violates most platform ToS. This guide only automates the notification side.

Step-by-step Guide

1

Identify your keywords & skills

Write down 5–10 search terms that describe the work you can do. Examples: react developer, python automation, wordpress plugin, logo design.

Specific keywords get fewer but better-matched results. Broad keywords get more noise. Start specific.

or write your keywords in a text file
2

Get RSS feed URLs from each platform

All major platforms offer RSS feeds for search results. Replace YOUR+KEYWORD with your search term (URL-encoded):

  • Upwork: https://www.upwork.com/ab/feed/jobs/rss?q=YOUR+KEYWORD&sort=recency
  • Freelancer: https://www.freelancer.com/rss/jobs/YOUR+KEYWORD.xml
  • PeoplePerHour: https://www.peopleperhour.com/freelance-jobs?_format=rss&keyword=YOUR+KEYWORD

Test your feed by opening the URL in a browser — you should see XML with job listings.

or copy the URLs above manually
3

Choose a notification method

Pick one or more of these free options:

  • RSS reader with alerts — Feedly, Inoreader, or the free RSSnoti.fy service sends an email on each new item
  • IFTTT / Make (formerly Integromat) — free plans can monitor RSS and send push notifications or emails
  • Self-hosted n8n — open source workflow tool, poll RSS every 5 minutes, send Telegram/Slack/email
  • GitHub Actions — free CI server that can poll RSS on a schedule and email you
or choose a tool from the list above
4

Set up RSS-to-email with IFTTT (no code)

IFTTT is the easiest no-code option. Create an Applet: Trigger: New item in RSS feed → Action: Send me an email.

  1. Go to ifttt.com/create
  2. Click If This → search "RSS Feed" → "New feed item"
  3. Enter your Upwork/Freelancer RSS URL
  4. Click Then That → search "Email" → "Send me an email"
  5. Save and enable the Applet
or follow the steps above manually
5

Respond fast & write strong proposals

When you receive an alert, open the job immediately. A great proposal:

  • Starts with a specific reference to their project (not a template)
  • Shows 1–2 relevant past projects or skills
  • Ends with a clear next step ("I can start Monday")
  • Is under 200 words

AI tools like Claude or ChatGPT can help draft a first proposal which you then personalise — cutting writing time from 20 minutes to 5 minutes.

or write your proposals manually
